Outrun the wolf. Eat grass. Don't stop.
Keyboard: Space = Play · N = Nightmare
How to Play

⬅️ ➡️ / A D, or swipe left/right: change lane

⬆️ / W / Space, or swipe up: jump

🪨 Rocks can be jumped. 🚧 Fences can't, so dodge them!

🌿 Eat grass to push the wolf back. Every hit lets it catch up.

🌙 Nightmare: darkness, a bigger and faster wolf, tougher rules.
